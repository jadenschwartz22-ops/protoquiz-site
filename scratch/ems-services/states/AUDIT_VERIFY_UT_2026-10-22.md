# UT verify audit, 2026-10-22 (20 A audited: 17 PASS, 3 FAIL)
Method: re-opened Medicaid 2026Q2 assessment (every quoted provider line and count found), UFA transport page, SLCPD 10/21/2025 release, auditor 2024 PDF, Dutch John notice, NPS Zion blog, St George News 2013/2015, Gold Cross/Utah Business 5/18/2026 release (all quotes found). Re-ran UGRC response-area overlay (polygon names per county) and the Bureau of EMS roster (every ground licence per county). Border slivers under ~5% treated as noise per prior audit.
- Salt Lake: PASS. 10 UGRC agencies all Medicaid transporters; SLC = Gold Cross (auditor 2024 + SLCPD 2025). Summit Vista #1872D is "without Transport Capability" on the roster. Caveat: Gold Cross SLC #1812L is typed "Medical Transport (Interfacility)" on the roster.

- Davis: PASS. Hill AFB on-base, Chevron refinery. Search: 2022 county-to-city shift done, nothing since.
- Washington: FAIL. UGRC "Cedar City Ambulance" 5.0% (New Harmony) not in row, operator only inferred as Gold Cross.
- Iron: FAIL. Parowan/Beryl polygons assumed Gold Cross; no page names them (2015 sale article, search found nothing).
- Summit: FAIL. South Summit roster #2204L non-transport; North Summit roster "without transport"; KPCW 12/2023: South Summit used Wasatch Fire for EMS in 2024, ending 2025; current transporter unproven.
- Uintah: PASS. Gold Cross Eastern/Ute Tribe/UBMC polygons + Medicaid; Deseret G&T #2402L power-plant ambulance (0.1% plant polygon).
- Wasatch: PASS. Search: Wasatch Fire minutes 12/2024, no change.
- Sanpete: PASS. 4 licences = 4 polygons; search: no merger.
- Carbon: PASS. carbon.utah.gov: county ambulance everywhere except East Carbon "who maintains their own service".
- Emery: PASS. Grand: PASS (Lower Valley FD CO 1.1% border strip). Kane: PASS. Garfield, Rich, Wayne, Piute: PASS (single licence = 100% polygon, no change found).
- Beaver: PASS. beaver.utah.gov SSD#3: "Milford Memorial Hospital and Ambulance Service provides health care to the residents of western Beaver County" (confirms the Medicaid MILFORD MEM HOSP line).
- Juab: PASS. Rocky Ridge EMS is a roster QRU (non transport); Gold Cross #2510L interfacility.
- Millard: PASS. Intermountain Power Service Corp #1404L is a power-plant ambulance, not in the UGRC layer.
