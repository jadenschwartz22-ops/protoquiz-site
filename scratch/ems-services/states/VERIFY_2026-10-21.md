# Verification spot check, 2026-10-21

Method: for each county, the 911 EMS provider named by the state file was checked against the county's or agency's own site, ordinance or board page (not the GIS layer the rows cite). curl with browser User-Agent, Chrome MCP read-only in one tab (closed). No WebSearch, no forms. Verdicts: CONFIRMED / PARTIAL / CONTRADICTED / NO SOURCE. Time-boxed to about 30 minutes, so each county got a few sources, not an exhaustive read.

## NE (3 counties; Douglas not tested, it is the file's own partial)

Overall: weak. 0 of 3 fully confirmed; 1 NO SOURCE. Labels are loose town/district names and the sources found do not tie them to transport.

**Lancaster (largest tier-A, pop 332,857): PARTIAL.** Layer labels are 16 town/district names (LINCOLN only 13.4% of area, 87% of area town-name labels).
- Lincoln city: "Pay Your Ambulance Bill Here ... If you have questions regarding your ambulance bill" and "EMS / Ambulance ... Lincoln Fire & Rescue's EMS Rates" (https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/Fire). Lincoln Municipal Code Title 7 "AMBULANCE TRANSPORTATION CODE", Ch. 7.08 Emergency Ambulance Service (http://online.encodeplus.com/regs/lincoln-ne/doc-viewer.aspx?secid=8036). So the LINCOLN row = Lincoln Fire & Rescue, a transporting agency. CONFIRMED for that row.
- Rural labels: Lincoln-Lancaster Mutual Aid page lists the 2026 hosts Southeast, Hallam, Valparaiso, Firth, Bennet, Malcolm, Hickman, Raymond, Southwest (https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/Fire/Mutual-Aid), consistent with the layer's town labels, but nothing there says who transports in rural Lancaster. Transport for the other 87% of area NOT confirmed.

**Buffalo (mid, pop 51,156): NO SOURCE.** Kearney Fire page (https://www.cityofkearney.org/524/Fire) describes fire protection only: "volunteer members of the Kearney Volunteer Fire Department ... Suburban Fire Protection District #1 of Buffalo and Kearney Counties"; no ambulance statement, and no source found naming GOOD SAM EMS (24% of area). Layer lists town VFDs (Amherst, Elm Creek, Gibbon, Pleasanton, Ravenna, Shelton, Sumner/Miller) that are fire departments by name; layer says nothing on transport.

**Thomas (rural, pop 634): PARTIAL.** Layer: THEDFORD AMBULANCE 95.8%, BLAINE COUNTY AMBULANCE 5.1%. Thedford's community site lists "Thedford Volunteer Fire & Rescue, 39253 NE-2, Thedford" (https://www.visitthedford.com/non-profits); Thomas County is dispatched by Region 26 (http://region26-911ne.gov/). The agency exists locally but under a Fire & Rescue name; no page found saying it carries patients or calling it an ambulance service.

Recommendation NE: wire with note. Keep "named loosely", no transport claim (rows already transport:false), and downgrade acronym/town-label counties (Sarpy is all acronyms: BVFD/GRFD/PAFD/SPFD) or hold them. Do not describe NE as independently verified.

## UT (3 counties)

Overall: provider names confirmed where checked; transport nuances for Salt Lake City and Daggett.

**Salt Lake (largest, pop ~1.2M): CONFIRMED for UFA, PARTIAL for Salt Lake City.**
- UFA: "UFA began providing 911 emergency ambulance services to all communities within UFA jurisdiction July 2009" and "exclusive license from the Bureau of Emergency Medical Services to provide 911 Ambulance Service" (https://unifiedfireut.gov/911-emergency-transport/). UFA also "first response and transport" (https://unifiedfireut.gov/about/). Layer: UFA 64.6%, transport yes. Matches.
- SLCFD (14.3%, layer says "Paramedic Rescue - ALS non transport"): SLC Fire says it provides "fire, rescue, and emergency medical services" (https://fire.slc.gov/). Gold Cross Ambulance lists "Transportation Partnership with Salt Lake City Fire Department" and "Primary and back up emergency 9-1-1 ground services", with a Salt Lake City/County base at 762 S. Redwood Road (http://goldcrossambulance.com/ -> https://goldcrossservices.com/service-areas). So the layer's non-transport flag for SLCFD is right, but the transporting 911 provider in the SLC area is Gold Cross, which the layer does not list. Add a note: SLC transport is by Gold Cross under partnership.
- Also from UFA: Draper City separated in 2017 and "provided its own ambulance transport service" (matches layer listing Draper FD as a ground ambulance).

**Uintah (mid, pop ~36k): PARTIAL.** Layer: Gold Cross Eastern 65.3%, Ute Tribe Ambulance 34.0%.
- Gold Cross lists a Uintah County base "220 West 100 South, Vernal, Utah 84078, (435) 789-6907" (https://goldcrossservices.com/service-areas) and "responds to calls in Salt Lake, St George, Uintah, Utah, Washington Iron, and Juab Counties" (https://goldcrossservices.com/about-us). Confirms Gold Cross operates in Uintah and transports; the page does not state its 911 role by area.
- Ute Tribe Ambulance: https://utetribe.com/ yielded no ambulance page; NO SOURCE for that 34%. uintah.utah.gov is a script-rendered site; no EMS page readable.

**Daggett (rural, pop ~600): PARTIAL (name/operator differs).** Layer: Flaming Gorge Fire and EMS District 99%.
- County's own EMS page: "Provide Emergency medical treatment and transport for sick and injured people in Daggett County and surrounding areas ... EMTs and Ambulance drivers are paid volunteers" under the county's "Emergency Medical Services" department (https://www.daggettcounty.gov/98/Ambulance-Services; FAQ https://www.daggettcounty.gov/134/Emergency-Medical-Services-FAQs). Emergency Services hub: "Staffed by Basic and Intermediate certified EMTs" (https://www.daggettcounty.gov/18/Emergency-Services).
- Flaming Gorge Fire and EMS District page: "Provide fire protection for western Daggett County" (https://www.daggettcounty.gov/100/Manila-Fire-Department); Dutch John FD "providing services to the Eastern part of Daggett County" (https://www.daggettcounty.gov/101/Dutch-John-Fire-Dept).
- So the county runs the ambulance as a county department; the district is fire for the west, Dutch John FD for the east. A 99% single-district label overstates it; no CONTRADICTION of a different transporter, but the operator is the county EMS.

Recommendation UT: wire with note. Label "from the state NG911 layer" (already in meta). Add: SLC transport by Gold Cross; Daggett county EMS operates the ambulance; Ute Tribe Ambulance unverified. Utah County (Eagle Mountain/Mapleton unmatched) not tested.
