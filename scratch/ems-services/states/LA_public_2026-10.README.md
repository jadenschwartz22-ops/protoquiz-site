## Verdict: NO parish is tier-A supportable from this
The page says Acadian serves the parish, not that it is the 911 provider or which part. All 39 rows are `partial` / basis `located`; the county_verdict leaves them for the master to decide (they will move none -> partial/B, not A). Known counter-signals, both from the parishes' own pages: Orleans (nola.gov lists an Office of Emergency Medical Services) and Bossier (bossierparishla.gov links parish EMS at bossierparishems.com). Treat large metros (EBR, Caddo, Jefferson, Orleans, Lafayette) as unconfirmed.
