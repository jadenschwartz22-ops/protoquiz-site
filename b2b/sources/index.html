Protocol Sources

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ProtoQuiz Agency is an independent training tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency. This page says where the protocol content in the app comes from and links to the original sources.

1. Where protocol content comes from

Every agency in the app uses its own protocol book: the document its medical director and agency have adopted. ProtoQuiz builds quizzes, scenarios and search from that book. We do not write, change or approve clinical protocols.

Members can open their agency's protocol book at any time from Profile → Source inside the app. The agency's book is the original source for everything the app teaches from it.

2. Original source for the demonstration agency

The demonstration agencies in the app use the Denver Metropolitan Prehospital Protocols, written and published by the Denver Metro EMS Medical Directors.

  • Original source: https://dmemsmd.org (Denver Metro EMS Medical Directors)

The protocol document states that its content is the property of the Denver Metro EMS Medical Directors and its contributors. ProtoQuiz does not claim authorship of it.

3. Using the app

ProtoQuiz is an educational training tool, not medical advice. Always follow your agency's approved protocols and your medical director's direction. If you find a mistake or an out-of-date page, tell your training officer, or write to support@protoquiz.com.
