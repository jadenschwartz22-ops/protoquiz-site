Remove Your ProtoQuiz Agency Account

Applies to: ProtoQuiz Agency for iOS and Android and the web app at youragency.protoquiz.com, published by ProtoQuiz LLC · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Your agency creates and manages your account. The ProtoQuiz Agency app has no sign-up. Your training officer adds you to the agency roster, and you sign in with your work email or phone. Removing the account works the same way: your agency removes you, or you ask us.

How to have your account removed

  1. Ask your training officer. An agency administrator removes you from the roster from the admin console. That ends your access and queues your personal data for deletion.
  2. Or email us. Write to support@protoquiz.com from the address on your account, or include your agency and the phone number on file. We confirm the request, then remove the account within 30 days.

In the app, Profile shows this same note under Sign out. Deleting the app from your device does not remove your account.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your account (email address, phone number, sign-in record)Deleted
Your profile (name, certification level, certification numbers)Deleted
Quiz attempts, scores, progress and leaderboard entriesDeleted
Feedback and survey responses you sentDeleted

What your agency keeps

DataRetention
Training records your agency has already signed (your name, certification number and the hours or sessions credited)Kept by the agency as its continuing-education record, for up to five years, as its contract with us and its own regulations require. Your email address and phone number are not part of that record.
Agency audit entries (who approved or removed whom, and when)Kept by the agency
Crash reports and server logs (not linked to your identity)Up to 90 days, then deleted automatically
Database backupsUp to 30 days, then overwritten
