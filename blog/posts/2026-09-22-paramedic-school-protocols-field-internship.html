ProtoQuiz Is THE Tool for Paramedic Students

By Jaden • September 22, 2026

In paramedic school you have to learn the full protocol set for the service you ride with: every drug, every protocol. It is not the same as the NREMT material from class, and it is what your preceptor grades you on. Most students do not have it down.

Upload that protocol and ProtoQuiz builds your studying from it. Every answer cites the page it came from.

  • Pharmacology. Med Mastery drills every drug field by field: doses, routes, indications, contraindications, mechanism, adverse effects. Hard mode drops the multiple choice, makes you type it, and grades it against your protocol.
  • Algorithms. Quizzes on the decision points: what criteria come first, what comes next.
  • Scenarios. A call runs in stages and you decide each step, scoped to your certification level.

Medicine Mode covers the pathophysiology underneath, and NREMT mode is there free when the registry exam comes up. EMT and AEMT students, medics working through a protocol revision and new hires use it the same way. Paramedic school is what it was built for.

New accounts get seven days of full Premium, no card. After that the free tier stays free: one protocol and three of each generated feature a day. Start with the drug list.

Your protocol and your preceptor always win. This is a study tool, not medical direction.
