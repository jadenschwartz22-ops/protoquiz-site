ProtoQuiz Update: NREMT Prep Is Now Included - and It's Free

By Jaden • June 24, 2026

Update, Sept 2026: ProtoQuiz no longer shows ads. The free tier now includes 3 per day of each generated feature.

I studied for my NREMT paramedic exam with ProtoQuiz, and I passed. So I built that out properly: ProtoQuiz now has a full NREMT prep section, and like everything else in the app, it's free. No question caps, no "unlock the full bank" paywall, no trial timer.

What's In It

  • 1,700+ questions across the EMT and Paramedic blueprints. Flip the level toggle and the bank, dashboard and simulator switch with you.
  • Per-option rationales. Every question explains why the right answer is right and why each of the other three is wrong. Most free banks skip that, and it's the part that teaches.
  • Mapped to the current NREMT blueprint - airway and ventilation, cardiology, trauma, medical and OB/GYN, EMS operations - so you can drill by domain.
  • A missed-question bank that recycles what you got wrong until you get it right, then retires it.
  • Question of the Day and a study streak for the days you only have two minutes.

The Readiness Dashboard

The part I'm proudest of. It breaks your performance down by domain and points you at your weakest area with a one-tap drill. And it won't lie to you: the "are you ready" verdict stays locked until you've answered enough questions for it to mean something.

The Adaptive Exam Simulator

The real NREMT is computer-adaptive: harder when you're doing well, easier when you're not, and it stops once it's confident. The ProtoQuiz simulator runs the same way - a variable-length exam of roughly 70 to 120 items that tracks your ability and estimates pass or fail. It's the closest thing to test day you can practice on, and it's the feature paid apps charge the most for.

Versus the Paid Apps

On what matters for passing, ProtoQuiz is right there with Pocket Prep and the other big names, and ahead on per-option explanations and Paramedic pathophysiology and ECG depth. Their edge is drag-and-drop and ordered-list items, which are on my roadmap. Use it as a supplement to your course material, not a replacement.

Why Free

Same reason as everything else in the app. The people who need NREMT prep most - students, career-changers, people testing on a tight timeline - are the ones least able to drop $40 on a study app.

Coming Next: Medicine Mode

Next up is a way to stay current on the medicine itself: the textbook you never got around to reading, done for you. The conditions you see on the truck, the pathophysiology in plain language, the evidence behind the treatment, and a built-in glossary. It even follows the case into the hospital, so you finally see what happens after you wheel the patient through the doors. It lands in an upcoming release.

Go Try It

Open ProtoQuiz, go to the Learn hub, and tap NREMT. Pick your level, run a custom quiz or jump into the simulator. No upload needed. If a question reads wrong or an explanation could be clearer, file feedback in the app, comment below, or email me. That's how this bank gets better.
