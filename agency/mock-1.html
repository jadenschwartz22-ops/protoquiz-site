Common questions
How does setup work?

Send us your protocol PDF. We build the question bank, scoped per certification level, every question verified against your document and cited to the page. You review it before it goes to your crews. We host on a custom subdomain; crews open a link in any browser. PDF to live tenant: about a week.

What if our protocols change mid-term?

Send the new PDF. We update the content, you review the changes, and your crews see the new questions on their next session.

How much IT lift is this?

None. No install, no SSO required (supported if you want it).

How does billing work?

$25 per seat, per quarter. Prepay the year and it is $80 per provider instead of $100. No setup fees, no add-ons, no tiers. The free six-month pilot program is closed; standard pricing applies to new agencies.
