Why Line 1 is a person and Line 3a is not "LLC." Teach Me to Live LLC is a single-member LLC treated as a disregarded entity for federal tax purposes. The IRS instructions for Form W-9 direct that for a disregarded entity, the owner's name goes on Line 1 and the entity name on Line 2, and that "a disregarded entity should instead check the appropriate box for the tax classification of its owner." The Line 3a "LLC" box accepts only C, S, or P — there is no code for a disregarded entity — so checking it would be incorrect. The EIN in Part I is the LLC's, which is the combination the IRS expects and the one that clears TIN matching.
