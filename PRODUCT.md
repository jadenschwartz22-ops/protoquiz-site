# PRODUCT.md — protoquiz.com marketing site

register: brand

## Product purpose
ProtoQuiz trains EMS providers on the one thing they use every shift: their own agency's protocols. Two audiences on one site: B2C (App Store app for individual EMTs/medics) and B2B (`/agency`, multi-tenant platform sold to EMS agencies). The full positioning source of truth is `VALUE_PROP.md` in this repo; it wins over anything here or on the live site.

## Users
- **B2B buyer (the /agency page):** EMS training officers, medical directors, QA chiefs. Skeptical, budget-conscious, allergic to vendor hype. They sign the deal and watch the dashboard.
- **B2B end user:** EMTs, AEMTs, paramedics on shift. Blunt, practical, insider vocabulary (FTO, QA, scope, protocols).

## Brand and tone
Hybrid voice: blunt medic insight, professional delivery. "EMS lags behind. Let's fix it." Never corporate hype ("revolutionize", "#1 platform", "best-in-class"), never founder-bro. First-person plural + second person ("we built", "your crews", "your protocols"). Full use/avoid word lists in VALUE_PROP.md §9–10. No emojis anywhere.

## Established aesthetic (do not redesign unprompted)
The /agency page is a settled editorial direction: warm cream/parchment ground, oversized serif display headlines, JetBrains Mono eyebrows/labels, near-black (#0a0a0a-family) product mockups (phone + laptop frames) with an autoplaying 8-beat demo, red clinical accents inside the mock (EKG card). Polish within it; don't swap the direction.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient heroes, icon card grids, fake testimonial walls).
- NREMT-prep / exam-cram framing (wrong audience for /agency).
- Anything claiming CE credit, "prevents lawsuits", or unsourced savings numbers — pricing/savings claims must match VALUE_PROP.md §8a exactly (pilot $50/provider/yr locked for life, standard $100, pilot closes Aug 1 2026 or when the last slot fills).

## Strategic principles
- Lead with the wedge: "your actual protocols", page-cited, scoped to cert level, medical-director sign-off.
- Real product proof beats claims: live demo mock, real EKG, real dashboard.
- Respect competitors; the wedge is what we do, not what they don't.
- Every section earns its keep; redundant sections get cut, not restyled.
