▸ About ProtoQuiz

Built by a working paramedic.

I'm Jaden — a paramedic and the person who builds ProtoQuiz™. I spent my EMS career so far in Denver, Colorado, and I just moved to Georgia.

ProtoQuiz started during my paramedic school field internship. I needed to learn the protocols I'd actually be using on shift — the local drugs, doses, and decision points — and every study tool I could find was generic NREMT prep. The other option was making flashcards from scratch, hundreds of pages of them. I knew there had to be a better way. So I built it.

What ProtoQuiz is

One idea runs through all of it: EMS should train on the protocols it actually works under. ProtoQuiz does that in three ways.

The app, for EMTs and paramedics on iOS and Android. Upload your agency's protocol PDF and get quizzes on pharmacology, algorithms and adaptive scenarios. Every answer cites the page it came from, so you can check it against the source. Extraction runs about 90% accurate in our testing, which is why you can edit anything it pulls.

For agencies, a training platform built on the agency's own protocols. Crews take one short quiz per shift, training officers see which topics need work, and the time is logged for your CE record. You are live 7 days after we receive your protocol.

Research, a free and open reference on how EMS differs across the US. The Protocol Census compares what agencies carry and how they dose it, and the American EMS Atlas maps who runs 911 EMS in every county. Only public agencies are named, and it is not for sale.
