Delete Your Account and Data

Applies to: ProtoQuiz (EMS ProtoQuiz) for iOS and Android, published by Teach Me to Live LLC · Last updated: August 26, 2026

ProtoQuiz has no sign-up. The app creates an anonymous account for you automatically on first launch — you never provide a name, email address, or password. Deleting that anonymous account removes the data tied to it.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app, on either platform:

  1. Open ProtoQuiz on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion begins immediately. Your anonymous account is removed and you are signed out; the app returns to its first-launch state.

If you can no longer open the app

Deleting the app from your device does not delete your data from our servers. If you have already uninstalled ProtoQuiz, the simplest route is to reinstall it and use the Profile screen — your anonymous account is restored on the same device, so the delete option will remove the right record.

If that is not possible, email support@protoquiz.com and we will help. Please note that because accounts are anonymous and hold no name or email address, we may need additional details from you — such as your approximate first-use date and the protocol document you uploaded — to identify your record with confidence.

Subscriptions must be cancelled separately

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not by us, so we cannot cancel one on your behalf.

Cancel before deleting your account:

  • Android: Play Store › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › ProtoQuiz › Cancel.
  • iOS: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › ProtoQuiz › Cancel Subscription.
